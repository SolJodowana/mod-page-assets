Dynamic magic weather for the Overworld! Brave auroras, blood moons, meteor showers, and seasonal storms to uncover Astralite, master Astral Magic, power Ley Pylons, and boost your mana.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge) and 1.20.1 (Forge).

🛡️ Build-Safe Guarantee: Meteor craters only affect soft natural ground (dirt, grass, sand, gravel, snow, clay) and will never spawn near chests, machines, or water. Lightning strikes do not ignite fires by default, and Astral spells never break blocks.

  • 7 Seasonal Phenomena: Mana Storms, Arcane Blizzards, Phantom Fog, Meteor Showers, Auroras, Blood Moons, and Ley Surges — each with custom skies, ambient sounds, and exclusive rewards.
  • Astral Magic School: Craft celestial spells (Meteor Strike, Comet Dash, Star Ward) and discover rare scrolls (Starfall, Gravity Well, Cosmic Burn) from weather hazards.
  • Gear & Materials: Mine Astralite from craters to craft 80% hazard-resistant armor, Meteorite Ingots, and Astral Cores for high-tier magic gear.
  • Ley Line Pylons & Pendants: Harness natural ley lines across a 24-block field and equip linked pendants to expand your maximum mana and boost mana recovery.
  • Forecast & Tracking: Check real-time weather, seasons, and multi-day forecasts using the Barometer, HUD Hotbar icons, in-game cards, or /tempestalmanac forecast.
  • Visual & Audio Depth: Native support for Iris Shaders (Complementary Reimagined) and Lodestone Engine (camera shake & soft glow particles).
