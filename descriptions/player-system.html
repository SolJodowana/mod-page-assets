Awaken your System, level up your stats, fuse unique skills, and clear deadly dungeons, gates, and a Tower as a rising Hunter! Brings a full manhwa-inspired RPG progression layer to Minecraft guided by a floating holographic 3D interface.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge) (Java 21 required) and Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge) (Java 17 required, beta).

🚧 Forge 1.20.1 is a beta and a work in progress. It may be unstable, so please report any problems in the comments or on GitHub. Back up your world first: worlds cannot be moved between 1.20.1 and 1.21.1.

⚡ Activation Trigger: Your character must die once to awaken the System and roll your innate rank (E to SSS). Works safely and seamlessly in Hardcore mode without losing your world!

  • Holographic 3D System Window: Press I to access your personal window (Status, Class, Skills, Quests, Shop, Tower, Wiki, Options) with customizable skins, opacity, and server ranking visibility.
  • Classes & Rank Aura: Pick a class line for each attribute, each with its own skills and evolutions, and wear a rank aura that can be switched off in the config.
  • Rank Awakening & Stat Allocation: Roll your innate rank to set stat caps. Freely allocate points to health, attack power, speed, and mana with full respec support.
  • 150+ Skills, Fusions & Plunder: Train skills to ascend them, fuse abilities in the Synthesis Tab, or use Skill Plunder (SSS/EX) to siphon abilities directly from mobs and bosses.
  • Iron's Spells Skills: Cast a spell from a book, scroll or sword for the first time and you learn a Spell skill for it, one per spell (107 spells). It is paid from your System mana, runs on its own cooldown, and shows up in the Skills tab; channelled spells end by themselves after at most 10 seconds. Turn it off with ironsSpellSkills = false in player_system-common.toml.
  • Gates, Dungeons & The Tower: Clear stylized Gates (Rifts, Sigils, Pylons) and conquer a 10+ floor Keep with party support, anti-farm mechanisms, and protected loot drops.
  • Ranked Mobs & Mob Skills: Mobs spawn with randomized ranks, affixes, and active/passive skills (up to 6 for dungeon bosses). Earn open-world XP with built-in anti-farm limits.
  • Quests, System Shop & Titles: Accept daily/procedural quests, earn System Coins for emergency supplies, and unlock stat-boosting titles on server leaderboards.
  • Instant Hub Teleportation: Access the Hub using a hotkey, UI button, or /hub (disabled during combat and dungeon runs).

📌 Requirements

  • GeckoLib (Required | Enables animated 3D models; 4.8+ on 1.20.1)
  • Forge 1.20.1 (beta): Forge 47.4.10+, Java 17. Not included yet: the Cataclysm dungeons. Six skills have no special effect there.

  • Quintessence: Elemental Spellcraft & Runes — Shared mana pool compatibility and elemental progression hooks.
  • Tempest Almanac — Special weather challenges and seasonal system quest goals.

🧩 Optional Mod Integrations

  • Mana Systems: Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, Ars Nouveau (both use native pool mana to power System skills)
  • Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks: Spell skills (above), ranks for its spell books, rings, armor and staffs, 73 of its materials as dungeon loot, and nine of its mobs as ranked dungeon species: Catacombs Zombie, Necromancer, Magehunter Vindicator, Archevoker, Cultist, Ice Spider, Citadel Keeper and the bosses Dead King and Fire Boss (Iron's Spells 3.16.3). Without Iron's the spell skills stay in your list, greyed out and unusable, until it is back.
  • Bosses & Structures: L_Ender's Cataclysm (mobs gain ranks/skills, bosses and structures populate Tower floors & Gate dungeons; 1.21.1 only for now)
  • Universal Support: Hostile mobs from any mod yield XP, and equipment automatically receives item ranks based on attributes.

  • Datapack Driven: 100% extensible! Customize quests, skills, fusions, classes, and rewards via datapacks.
  • Developer API: Registrations for custom quest objectives, rewards, penalties, and external integration hooks.
