Statues that move only when nobody is watching.

Deep underground, amid the old stone of dungeons and mineshafts, stand statues of angels with their hands covering their faces. Some are harmless decoration. Some are not.

An angel remains frozen while any player can see it. Look away—even for a second—and it comes for you. It stalks through corridors, slips around corners in a blink, and grabs when it closes the distance. Keep your eyes on it, back away, and it can never touch you… until you have to turn around.

Supports Minecraft 26.1.2 (NeoForge) (Java 25 required).

An angel counts as observed when at least one eligible player:

  • Is within 24 blocks,
  • Has the angel inside a 100-degree view cone, and
  • Has an unobstructed line of sight to its body (opaque walls and closed doors block sight; glass does not).

While observed, an angel cannot move, teleport, or finish a grab—holding its motionless, face-covering pose with zero breathing, fluttering, or head movement.

  • Group Protection: Any eligible player keeping the angel in view freezes it for everyone nearby. It resumes moving only when nobody sees it.
  • Who Counts: Spectators never count, and Creative mode players do not count by default (configurable). Players afflicted with Blindness or Darkness cannot see it and do not count as observers.
  • Server-Authoritative: All observation checks and AI states are decided strictly on the server.

  1. Awakening: Wakes up when an eligible player enters its activation range without looking at it. Far from players, angels remain dormant with negligible overhead.
  2. Stalking: Pursues the nearest target—favoring players who are not actively watching—and commits to a target for a while rather than jittering between players.
  3. Teleportation: Teleports only when its path is blocked (and Advanced Angels also teleport to flank). Never lands inside a player's view, never within 4 blocks of any player, never inside blocks or liquids, and never forces chunk loads.
  4. The Grab: Within reach and with a clear line of sight, it begins a 1.2-second wind-up accompanied by an audible stone strain. Looking at the angel or stepping out of reach cancels the grab. If the wind-up finishes while unseen, the grab kills by default (bypasses armor, though Totems of Undying still save you), followed by a brief recovery cooldown.

Entity / Block Details & Behavior
Weeping Angel Statue Harmless 2-block-tall decorative statue on a stepped pedestal. Faces toward you when placed, breaks as a single block (pickaxe required), drops itself, and uses no block entity or tick logic. Very rarely a statue can come alive and turn into a living Weeping Angel (spawning.statueAwakenChance, default 0.001; set it to 0 to disable).
Standard Angel The default hunter: 16 awareness range, 12 activation range, normal speed, and teleports up to 8 blocks every 10 seconds when blocked.
Advanced Angel Rare and deadlier: 24 awareness range, 18 activation range, 1.5x speed, teleports up to 12 blocks every 7 seconds, and may flank. Appears slightly darker with no glow or extra warning.
  • Combat & Immunities: Angels have 100 HP, drop no loot, and are immune to fire, drowning, and suffocation.
  • Creative & Testing: Spawn eggs for both Standard and Advanced angels are available in the creative tab. They never spawn naturally in open caves or on the surface.

Statues generate exclusively underground during world generation:

  • Dungeons: 1–2 around the spawner room.
  • Abandoned Mineshafts: Sparse placements.
  • Ancient Cities: More frequent and more dangerous.
  • Strongholds: Rare placements.

Across ten placements, roughly seven are decorative statues, two or three are Standard Angels, and around one in twenty is an Advanced Angel. Each chunk caps out at two hostile angels (max one Advanced) while maintaining spacing between statues. Generation relies strictly on the chunk being generated—reloading chunks never duplicates statues, and existing worlds only receive statues in newly generated chunks.

  • Modpack Structure Tag: Add structure IDs to weepingangels:extra_underground to place statues inside modded underground structures.

Configure config/<world>/weepingangels-server.toml (automatically synced to clients):

  • Observation & Variants: Observation range, view cone angle, Creative observer toggle, plus per-variant awareness, activation range, speed multiplier, teleport range/cooldown, and teleportation toggle.
  • Grab & Lethality: Wind-up duration, cooldown, lethal grab toggle, and non-lethal damage amount.
  • Worldgen, Audio & Debug: Generation toggle, decorative/standard/advanced weights, per-structure toggles, minimum statue spacing, hostile per-chunk cap, sound volume, and state/placement debug logging.

  • Client & Server: Install on both the server and every client.
  • Atmospheric Audio: Uses quiet stone scrapes and creaks—no jump-scare stingers, music, or wall-piercing radar cues.
  • Survival Tips:
    • Never turn your back on a statue you haven't verified.
    • Putting a wall, pillar, or closed door between you and an angel blocks your line of sight too—letting it move.
    • Watch from multiple angles in multiplayer, and keep lighting in mind so darkness doesn't hide an approaching threat.
