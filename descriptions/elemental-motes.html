Small, cheerful elemental creatures drifting freely through your Minecraft world! Naturally curious and neutral, these motes observe players peacefully unless provoked.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge).

  • 5 Elements & 4 Sizes: Meet Fire, Water, Earth, Air, and Aether motes in Tiny, Normal, Big, and Giant sizes (larger motes drop more loot but deal higher damage).
  • 15 Variants (new in 1.1): Every element now has three recoloured variants: Magma, Plasma, Ember, Frost, Abyssal, Swamp, Moss, Sand, Obsidian, Storm, Lightning, Zephyr, Void, Star and Aurora. They look different and behave like their element.
  • Elemental Reactions: Hitting a mote with its own element heals it and causes it to grow for 1 minute. Striking it with an opposing element (Fire vs Water, Earth vs Air) deals double damage!
  • Unique Habitats: Motes spawn naturally in their elements (Fire in hot biomes, Water in rivers/rain, Earth in caves, Air high up, Aether near geodes and high altitudes at night).
  • Mote Charms: Craft companion charms to summon an immortal elemental follower providing passive buffs (Fire: ignites mobs, Water: Water Breathing, Earth: Haste, Air: Slow Falling, Aether: item magnet).
  • Elemental Lanterns: Place specialized light sources with passive utility (Fire: stops undead spawns, Air: repels creepers/phantoms, Water: keeps farmland moist, Earth: boosts crop growth).
  • Mote Field Guide: In-game book with dedicated pages, tooltips, and advancements (translated in English and Polish).

Each of the five elements has three recoloured variants. A variant uses the model, animations and behaviour of its element (same reactions, drops, charms and lantern link); only the look and the particle colour change.

  • Fire: Magma, Plasma, Ember
  • Water: Frost, Abyssal, Swamp
  • Earth: Moss, Sand, Obsidian
  • Air: Storm, Lightning, Zephyr
  • Aether: Void, Star, Aurora

Natural spawns, spawn eggs and spawners roll a variant with the server setting spawn.variantChance (default 0.3; 0 turns variants off). To get one directly: /summon elemental_motes:mote ~ ~ ~ {MoteKind:"fire",MoteVariant:"magma"}
