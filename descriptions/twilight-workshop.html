Twilight Workshop is a progression addon for The Twilight Forest that introduces six new tiers of armor, tools, and weapons bridging the gap between native Twilight metals and its deadliest bosses.

Earn six distinct materials directly from forest mobs, dungeons, and boss encounters, weave them together with classic Twilight Forest ingots at the Workshop Bench, and forge full sets of gear for every stage of your expedition.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge) and Minecraft 26.1.2 (NeoForge).

⚠️ Early Release (v0.1.0): Ore generation, specialized weapon types, and armor set bonuses are not part of this initial release. Always back up your worlds before updating!

Each tier includes a complete 9-piece loadout (sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, helmet, chestplate, leggings, and boots — 54 items in total) alongside a dedicated storage block and distinct visual palette. All gear can be enchanted, repaired with its tier material, and customized with armor trims.

There is no worldgen ore mining in v0.1.0—every resource is earned through exploration and combat across the Twilight Forest:

  • Tier 1 — Mossbone Shard: Dropped by forest mobs (Hedge Spiders, Hostile Wolves, Redcaps, Swarm Spiders) and found inside Knight Stronghold chests.
  • Tier 2 — Cairnstone: Found in Hollow Hill chests and dropped by Kobolds and Beetles. Smelt Raw Cairnstone into a Cairnstone Chunk.
  • Tier 3 — Gloamsteel: Found in Dark Tower caches and dropped by Wraiths, Skeleton Druids, Mist Wolves, Mosquito Swarms, and Slime Beetles. Smelt Raw Gloam into an ingot.
  • Tier 4 — Aurorite Gem: Found in Aurora Palace chests and dropped by Yetis, Winter Wolves, Snow Guardians, and Ice Crystals.
  • Tier 5 — Wyrmsteel: Found in Troll Vault and Troll Garden chests (Raw Wyrm), or obtained from the Hydra (drops a Wyrm Heart, which converts into three Raw Wyrm at the bench). Smelt Raw Wyrm into an ingot.
  • Tier 6 — Evenfall Crystal: Dropped by the Twilight Lich, Ur-Ghast, and Snow Queen. Combine two crystals to craft an Evenfall Core for top-tier equipment.

  • Crafting: Combine a Crafting Table and an Iron Ingot.
  • Twilight Integration: Functions as a dedicated 3x3 crafting station exclusively for Workshop gear. Rather than replacing Twilight Forest's progression, recipes require classic Twilight Forest ingots as binding agents:
    • Tier 2 (Cairnstone): Ironwood Ingot
    • Tier 3 (Gloamsteel): Steeleaf
    • Tier 4 (Aurorite): Knightmetal Ingot
    • Tiers 5 & 6 (Wyrmsteel & Evenfall): Fiery Ingot
  • Datapack Friendly: Uses standard recipe formats wherever possible, making it easy for modpack creators to tweak or remove recipes via datapacks.
