Pick any mob and play as it! Mob Origins for NeoOrigins gives every mob in the game its own playable origin—complete with its real model, textures, hitbox, and sounds, paired with custom abilities and drawbacks. Fly as a ghast, blink as an enderman, split as a slime, summon fangs as an evoker, or simply live the quiet life of a very sturdy cow.

This is an addon for NeoOrigins (the NeoForge port of Origins). All available origins appear right inside the standard origin picker alongside base NeoOrigins choices.

Supports Minecraft 26.1.2 (NeoForge) (Java 25 required).

⚠️ Work in Progress (v0.0.1): Balance numbers are initial estimates and the mod has seen limited playtesting so far. Please back up your worlds before updating and share any feedback on what feels off!

  • 85 Playable Origins: Includes 33 passive, 18 neutral, 31 hostile, and 3 boss origins—with 82 selectable directly in the origin picker.
  • True Mob Models, Hitboxes & Sounds: Look, sound, and move like the creature you choose. Hitboxes scale with the origin so large mobs can still fit through doorways while tiny mobs feel genuinely small.
  • Abilities & Drawbacks: Each origin typically features three or four abilities alongside one defining drawback—mixing passive traits with active keybind abilities and cooldowns.
  • Diverse Mechanics: Teleportation, fireballs, flight, wall climbing, frog tongue grabs, remote button activation, block carrying, treasure sense, bartering, and more.

  • Enderman: Blink where you aim, carry blocks, and look at other endermen without provoking them—but beware of water.
  • Slime: Bounce off every block, split into a smaller slime at 8 HP, and take no fall damage.
  • Blaze: Full fire immunity, slow fall, and a three-fireball volley—weak to water.
  • Wolf: Howl to call nearby wolves, gain strength under a full moon, and grow empowered through taming and breeding.
  • Evoker: Summon evoker fangs, keep illagers neutral toward you, and occasionally save totems from being consumed.
  • Frog: Lash out with your tongue to pull targets in, jump higher, and ignore fall damage.
  • Many More: Includes Dolphin, Bee, Strider, Witch, Fox, Drowned, and dozens of other mobs across the roster.

Elder Guardian, Ender Dragon, and Wither are not selectable directly in the picker—instead, you earn them by defeating their respective boss while playing as a fitting origin:

  • Guardian → Elder Guardian: Defeat an Elder Guardian.
  • Enderman → Ender Dragon: Defeat the Ender Dragon.
  • Wither Skeleton → Wither: Defeat a Wither.

The transformation triggers upon completing the kill. If you already earned the underlying advancement before selecting the origin, you can reset it with /advancement revoke to trigger the upgrade.

📌 Requirements (Install on both Client & Server)

  • Minecraft: 26.1.2
  • NeoForge: 26.1.2 (Java 25 required)
  • NeoOrigins: 2.x

🛠️ Commands, Datapacks & Current Limitations

  • Origin Command: Use /neoorigins set @s neoorigins:origin mob_origins:<mob> to assign an origin directly (for example, mob_origins:cow).
  • Datapack Friendly: Origins and most powers are defined via standard data files and can be customized or overridden using datapacks.
  • Temporarily Omitted Mechanics: Certain abilities requiring changes to vanilla riding, passenger handling, shoulder perching, or powder snow walking are not yet active—specifically riding/carrying on you (Strider, Camel Husk, Happy Ghast), Parrot shoulder perching, and powder snow walking (Goat, Stray). Their base origins remain playable.
