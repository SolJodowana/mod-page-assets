Bestiary Almanac keeps a living record of every creature you encounter. Each mob starts as a mysterious dark card with a question mark; get a clear look to reveal its card, defeat it to learn its combat stats, and keep hunting to master it completely!

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge) and Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge).

⚠️ Early Development (0.0.x): This mod is under active development. Settings, configs, and saved data structures may still evolve between 0.0.x versions. Always back up your worlds before updating!

  • Automatic Mod Compatibility: Organizes creatures into chapters (Overworld, Nether, The End, plus dedicated chapters for every installed mod that adds living entities). Requires zero setup or custom code from other mod authors!
  • Collector Cards: Grid-based pages featuring real animated 3D models of each creature, current rank status, and kill counts. Unmet creatures appear as shrouded silhouettes.
  • Interactive 3D Drawer: Click any unlocked card to open a detailed drawer. Inspect a slowly rotating 3D model, drag to rotate it freely, and view habitat details, combat stats, and drops.
  • Robust Search & Filtering: Instantly search by name across all chapters, or toggle filters to display only creatures you have seen or defeated.
  • Intuitive Navigation: Seamlessly browse cards using keyboard arrow keys and turn pages with your mouse scroll wheel.

Rank Requirements What You Learn & Unlock
I. Seen Get a clear look at it (within 24 blocks) Where it lives (habitat biomes & structures)
II. Defeated 1 Kill Health, attack speed, armor, and drops you have actually witnessed
III. Veteran 25 Kills Combat traits and weaknesses (e.g., Smite vulnerability, fire immunity)
IV. Master 100 Kills Complete lore background and a permanent +10% damage bonus against that mob type!

Note: Boss creatures require 5x fewer kills to rank up. Every numerical threshold is fully configurable in the server settings.

You can access your Bestiary in four convenient ways:

  1. Codex Hub: Open with H (from the Arcane Framework) to see the book alongside your other Almanac volumes.
  2. Dedicated Hotkey: Bind a custom key in your Controls menu (Open the Bestiary).
  3. Inventory Screen Icon: Pin the book inside the Codex Hub, then drag its shortcut icon anywhere on your inventory screen.
  4. Physical Bestiary Item: Right-click the item in your hand. Craft it shapelessly from a Book and a Spyglass, or grab it from the creative Tools tab.

  • Real Habitats: Spawning data is read directly from active world generation lists, automatically including biomes and structures from datapacks and mods.
  • Dynamic Attributes: Stats are pulled straight from the game engine. No compatibility patches are needed for modded creatures to be fully tracked.
  • Custom Lore & Tags: Mods or resource packs can inject lore using a single lang key (bestiary_almanac.lore.<mod>.<creature>). Use tags like bestiary_almanac:hidden to exclude a mob or bestiary_almanac:boss to apply boss thresholds.
  • Server-Authoritative Progress: Progression is saved per player on the server side. Modified clients cannot cheat or unlock cards illegitimately, and player deaths do not reset your progress.
  • Spell Atlas Integration: If you are upgrading from Spell Atlas 2.0+, your previous magic mob kill counts are automatically imported into the Bestiary the first time you join a world (can be toggled off via importSpellAtlas).

  • /bestiary grant <players> <creature> <rank 0-4> — Instantly sets a creature's rank for specified players.
  • /bestiary import <players> — Re-runs the one-time Spell Atlas kill import.
  • /bestiary reset <players> — Clears all Bestiary progress.
