Bosses worth traveling for. Each Overlord comes with its own handcrafted arena, multi-phase mechanics, and custom boss health bar. This initial release introduces the first encounter: The Void Archon.

Supports Minecraft 26.1.2 (NeoForge) (Java 25 required).

⚠️ First Release (v0.1.0): Please back up your worlds before updating and share feedback on what feels off!

  • An Otherworldly Archmage: A floating, four-armed boss standing roughly 5.5 blocks tall—adorned with a fractured halo, spinning rune ring, six orbiting crystal shards, a cape of stars, and two floating spell tomes with emissive eyes and runes.
  • 400 HP & Pure Combat Focus: Immune to knockback, pushing, and non-player environmental cheese (fire, lava, suffocation, and bed explosions have zero effect).
  • Dormant & Arena-Bound: Sleeps silently at the center of its arena until a Survival player steps within 12 blocks. If all players leave the arena for 10 seconds, the boss resets to full health, Phase 1, and returns to sleep.

All damage values listed below apply to Normal difficulty.

Phase 1: The Awakening

  • Void Bolts: Fires a volley of 3 homing bolts every 3 seconds (6 damage each) that detonate on impact, piercing through cover.
  • Halo Sweep: After a 2-second telegraph, sweeps a sweeping beam across the entire arena for 14 damage—take cover behind the arena's stone pillars to block it.
  • Rune Ward: Striking the boss with two projectiles causes its rune ring to spin rapidly for 6 seconds, deflecting incoming arrows (melee attacks always bypass the ward).

Phase 2: Shattered Halo (Below 50% HP)

  • Shattering Shockwave: The Archon's halo fractures, unleashing a knockback shockwave that pushes nearby players back.
  • Orbiting Shard Blades: Its six crystal shards drop to body height and orbit at a 3.5-block radius, dealing 5 contact damage.
  • Tactical Blink: Teleports across the platform every 8 seconds—or immediately after taking three melee hits—telegraphing its destination with particles.
  • Escalated Barrages: Fires 5 homing Void Bolts per volley and sweeps two opposing beams simultaneously.

  • Renders a dedicated custom boss bar at the top of the screen featuring the Archon's name, a 50% phase threshold marker, and a dynamic shift to magenta upon entering Phase 2.
  • Visible exclusively to players actively inside the arena.

  • Outer End Generation: Generates as a ruined, 41-block-wide circular platform floating among the outer End islands (roughly half as common as End Cities).
  • Arena Layout: Six stone pillars provide cover against sweeping beams, while a fractured perimeter wall helps prevent knockback from sending you straight into the void.
  • Fair Engagement: Attacks originating from outside the arena are ignored—you must step inside to fight. Locate an arena manually with /locate structure overlords:shattered_observatory.
  • Current Rewards: In v0.1.0, defeating the Void Archon drops experience (200 XP)—unique boss drops and signature items are planned for upcoming updates.

  • Void Archon Loot: Custom drops, artifacts, and signature rewards.
  • Upcoming Overlords: Four more bosses, each with a dedicated arena and mechanics: Forge Warden, Thornroot Elder, Geode Stinger, and Tempest Knight.
