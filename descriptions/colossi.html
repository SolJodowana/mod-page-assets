Giant creatures carrying real, buildable land on their backs! Explore the world, construct mobile bases, ride lifts, and steer massive leviathans constructed from breakable blocks.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 on NeoForge and, since 0.97.1, on Fabric (separate file on the Files tab).

⚠️ Work in Progress: Colossi is under active development (the Scolopendra is currently WIP). Always back up your worlds before major updates!

  • The Zaratan: Giant tortoise carrying a massive 39,000-block island. Features screen-shaking footsteps and glowing runes.
  • The Jasconius: Deep-ocean leviathan holding an 80-block wrecked galleon with blowhole spouts and a lifeboat lift.
  • The Aurelia: Flying jellyfish carrying a 71-block round sky observatory inside a glass bell with gondola lifts and altitude controls.
  • The Scolopendra (WIP): Steampunk centipede train featuring a locomotive with 8 customizable cars for workshops and storage.
  • The Limax (new, early): A gel dome about 22 blocks wide, 24 long and 18 high that slides over the ground and floats on deep water. You can build in it and walk into it. Limax Egg and Limax Seed are in the Colossi creative tab. It is a first version that has not had a long play test yet.

  • Helm Controls: Direct third-person steering (W/S Accelerate/Brake, A/D Steer, Space Rest, C Lock Camera, Shift Exit). Special keybinds: R/V (Altitude), G (Anchor), R (Whale Breach).
  • Safe Construction & Terrain: Player builds and claimed chunks are completely safe. Crushes natural foliage, which regrows after 5 minutes.
  • Colossus Whistle: Shift-click the deck to pair, allowing you to summon your leviathan anywhere.
  • Mobile Chunk Loading: Built-in chunk loading (configurable 5x5 radius, server togglable) keeps your machines running and base loaded while traveling.
