Craft your own modular staffs! Forge parts from over 430 materials (340+ on Forge 1.20.1), assemble a custom staff tailored to your school of magic, salvage any staff you find, and fill an interactive Night Grimoire filled with quests, titles, and perks along the way.

Break arcane seals in hidden vaults, gamble on unidentified scrolls, draw spell glyphs, and infuse your gear with pure mana. An expansive addon for Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, integrated into the Arcane Framework Codex Hub (H) and designed to pair seamlessly with Spell's Atlas.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge) and Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge).

Build a custom staff at the Artificer's Table from four modular components: Core, Crystal, Shaft, and an optional Pommel.

  • 430+ Supported Materials: Pulls from vanilla, Iron's Spells, common metal tags, and dozens of mods (Twilight Forest, The Aether, Cataclysm, Create, Mekanism, Silent Gear, Mystical Agriculture, Ice and Fire, Botania, Ars Nouveau, and more). Materials appear dynamically only when their source mod is installed.
  • School-Shaped Cores: Forge cores from school-specific materials or combine any material with a school focus. The staff head visually morphs to match your school—forming claws, flames, ice shards, halos, crescents, or eldritch eyes.
  • Rarities, Affixes & Sets: Progress from Common to Legendary, Mythic, and Cataclysmic. Discover 36 affixes (school power, hybrids, and risky cursed modifiers), 21 material set bonuses, and 10 unique boss-dropped Legendary staffs.
  • Spell Imbuing & Upgrades: Write any Iron's Spells scroll directly into a core, and apply standard Arcane Anvil upgrades.
  • Four Table Tiers: Advance through Apprentice, Adept, Master, and Archmage tiers unlocked via Artificer's Notes.
  • Transparent Stats & Previews: All bonuses use clean attribute modifiers visible on every stats screen. Hold Shift to compare parts directly with your held staff and inspect a live preview before crafting.

  • Universal Staff Salvaging: Disassemble vanilla Iron's Spells staffs, addon staffs, and modular staffs at the Salvage Anvil to recover reusable parts and Arcane Dust while preserving Arcane Anvil upgrades on the core. Run /almanac salvage scan to inspect all recognized staffs in your modpack.
  • Mana Infusion & Aspect Charging: Pour mana into modular staffs or Manasteel gear using Manalite Shards (neutral charge) or School Runes (elemental charge) to lower mana costs, boost spell power, strengthen mana shields, and speed up tools.
  • Five-Level Resonance: Gear levels up as you use it—unlock up to five permanent Resonance tiers via risk-free table rituals.
  • Manalite Cells & Mana Condenser: Store personal mana and shards inside portable cells (three sizes). Carry cells in a pouch or Curios slot to automatically fuel spellcasting and infusion first.

  • Arcane Seals & Sealed Vaults: Solve magical puzzles guarding underground crypts and Sealed Caches (Lootr-compatible)—cast matching schools, repeat sequences, harmonize with friends, aim beams through mirrors, or echo spells with a Siphon. Failing triggers Mana Sickness and temporarily silences that school. Includes a craftable risk-free Practice Seal.
  • Abandoned Workshops: Discover surface ruins stocked with an Artificer's Table, Salvage Anvil, spare parts, and research notes.
  • Overcast & Spell Siphon: Push beyond zero mana into risky Overcast debt (tuned via Stability and Volatile crystals), or equip a Spell Siphon curio to absorb incoming spell projectiles and release stored charges.
  • Contingency Charms & Unidentified Scrolls: Equip automated charms that cast defensively when low on health, falling, burning, drowning, or surrounded, and either gamble by reading Unidentified Scrolls blind or safely decode them at the Arcane Lectern.

  • Runic Spell Glyphs: Draw traps and triggers on the ground with Runic Chalk, bind a spell scroll, and power them with Manalite Cells to fire on enemy proximity, redstone signals, or timers.
  • Arcane Wards: Deploy cell-powered area structures—Nullstone (anti-magic silence field), Wellspring (mana regeneration aura), and Aegis (spell damage reduction).
  • Manasteel Gear: Smelt deep-blue Manalite Ore (spawns across the Overworld, Nether, End, and modded dimensions) into mana-powered tools and armor featuring a 3x3 mining pickaxe and double jump. Upgrades into Starsteel when Tempest Almanac is installed.
  • Arcane Saddles: Four tiers of magical saddles capable of taming and riding any mob—including hostile monsters and bosses, turning them into loyal combat mounts.

Integrated into the Arcane Framework Codex Hub (H) alongside Spell's Atlas and Bestiary Almanac:

  • Collection & Mod Tracker: Browse all discovered materials grouped by source mod with completion bars, plus dedicated logs for affixes, seals, legendaries, and sets.
  • 30 Quests, 20 Titles & 9 Perks: Progress through five chapters from Apprentice to Archmage, unlock passive bonus titles, and purchase 9 Artificer perks (using Arcane Points if Spell's Atlas is installed, or Arcane Dust standalone).

📌 Required Mods (Install on both Client & Server)

  • Arcane Framework 0.0.1+ (Required library providing the Codex Hub and reader engine)
  • Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks 3.16+ (along with Curios API)

  • Iron's Spells Addons: Automatic salvage support, cores, and shaped staff heads for Cataclysm Spellbooks, Hazen N Stuff, Monsters & Spellbooks, Ace's Spell Utils, Wind's Spellbooks, and more.
  • Material Mods: Extensive forging support and cross-dimension Manalite ore generation for Twilight Forest, The Aether, Cataclysm, Create, Mekanism, Silent Gear, Mystical Agriculture, Ice and Fire, Embers, Biomes O' Plenty, Botania, Ars Nouveau, The Undergarden, Malum, Forbidden & Arcanus, Deeper and Darker, Alex's Caves, Galosphere, Occultism, and more.
