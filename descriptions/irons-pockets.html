Stop running back to base every time you want to swap a spell! Iron's Pockets adds two lightweight quality-of-life features for Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, letting you inscribe and remove spell scrolls on the go.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge).

  • Portable Inscription Kit: A handheld item that opens the authentic Iron's Spells Inscription Table interface anywhere—no placed block required. Right-click the item or press K (rebindable in Controls) while holding it in your inventory, either hand, or a Curios slot. Closing the screen safely returns your spellbook and scrolls to your inventory.
  • Inventory Spellbook Panel: Open your survival inventory to view a sleek side panel displaying every slot of your active spellbook.
    • Quick Inscribe: Pick up a scroll on your cursor and click an empty slot to insert it.
    • Quick Extract: Click an occupied slot with an empty cursor to pull the spell back out as a scroll.
    • Clear Indicators: Hover any slot to inspect the spell name and level; locked innate spells are clearly marked and cannot be extracted.
  • Smart Spellbook Detection: Automatically targets your equipped Curios spellbook slot first, falling back to your main hand and then your off-hand.

  • Portable Inscription Kit: Place an Inscription Table directly above a Stick in any crafting grid (consumes the Inscription Table).

  • Server-Authoritative & Safe: Every action is validated server-side under native Inscription Table rules—preventing duplicate spells, respecting locked spell slots, and allowing third-party mod events to cancel inscriptions cleanly.
  • Clean UI Behavior: Automatically renders beside your survival inventory and hides itself if screen space is tight or when the vanilla recipe book occupies that side.
  • Zero Save Lock-In: Uses native Iron's Spells spellbook NBT/data components directly—your spellbooks remain 100% vanilla-compatible if you ever remove the mod.
  • Scope: Applies to the survival inventory screen only (one-click spell swapping and creative inventory integration are intentionally omitted).
