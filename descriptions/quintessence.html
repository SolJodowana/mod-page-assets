Draw your spells, grow your power, and make magic your own! A standalone elemental magic mod for Minecraft featuring pentagram spell crafting, modular staffs, necromancy, and dimensional gates.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge).

⚠️ Early Development: Quintessence is under active development. Expect regular balance updates, fixes, and new content. Always back up your worlds before updating!

  • Pentagram Spell Crafting: Draw spells by connecting 5 core elements (Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Aether) inside your Grimoire to discover secret hybrid formulas.
  • Runes & Cores: Customise spell paths, shapes, timing, and purposed cores (Attack, Protection, Support, Control, Transport, Utility).
  • 3 Mana Paths: Awaken at an Altar or Wellspring to choose your mana path: Natural, Blood (greater power at the cost of mana debt), or Technical.
  • Skill Tree & 16 Pre-made Builds: Deep progression tree with spell library management, fusion, and sharing.
  • Modular Staffs & Gear: Forge customizable staffs, robes, wings, plate, and steampunk armor at the Artificer's Table.
  • Mana Infrastructure & Infrastructure: Mana cells, condensers, Manalite, Manasteel, Astralite, and Starsteel.
  • Dimensional Travel: Blink, recall, rift steps, permanent Astralite Gates, and a customizable pocket dimension home with windows and far gates.
  • Necromancy & World Hazards: Harvest souls, raise thralls, create Nemesis thralls, face weather anomalies (fog, meteors, Blood Moon), and summon familiars.
