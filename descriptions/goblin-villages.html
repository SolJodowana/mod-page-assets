Goblins have built villages in the swamps, mangrove swamps and dark forests of your world. Each one is a walled settlement with a campfire square, a bounty board, a totem, a smithy, a mushroom farm, a chief's den and burrows—and under the ground a network of tunnels that joins the goblins' dwellings. The goblins are neutral: they live, sleep, work and trade like villagers, and they remember how you treated them.

Supports Minecraft 26.1.2 (NeoForge). Requires Folkforge - Library (0.0.3 or newer).

✨ New in 0.1.0: a bounty board in every village, chief's commissions, hide armor, hunters' camps and goblins that walk home out of the rain.

Right-click the board at the plaza to see today's bounties. Each one asks for items (mushrooms, fish, bones, iron, spider eyes and the like) and pays goblin coins; the harder ones pay more. A finished bounty counts like a gift: it builds trust and lowers prices. The list is the same for everybody in the village and is picked again every game day. About twenty bounties are in the mod, and a datapack can add more (data/goblin_villages/bounty/*.json).

The chief's commission pays better: diamonds, ender pearls, experience bottles, or a piece of goblin armor. It is locked until you have trust 15 with the village, or while you wear the goblin crown (the crown drops from the chief). A village that is hostile to you does not open its board.

  • Hide armor: Four pieces in the goblin look: cap (1 armor), vest (4), kilt leggings (3) and boots (1), between leather and chain mail. They have no crafting recipes. The smith sells caps and boots from level 3 and vests and leggings from level 4, and a chief's commission may pay a vest or leggings. A full set gives +2 trust on every gift, trade and bounty (it adds to the crown's +5). Leather and bone repair it.
  • Hunters' camps: A small camp now and then in a swamp or a dark forest, never near a goblin village: a ring of low stakes, two hide tents, a campfire, a trader (a Scrapper), a Spearman and an Archer. The camp is a tiny village of its own. Its two chests hold coins, arrows, string, meat, bones and leather, sometimes a short bow or a spear. Open one and the camp turns on you; the villages stay calm.

Goblins trade in goblin coins. Five jobs, each on a vanilla job site, so no new job blocks:

Each job has five levels of offers. Giving a goblin an item it wants (crouch and right-click; every goblin takes goblin coins) is a gift: it builds trust and lowers prices.

Items: goblin coin, raw and cooked goblin meat, goblin spear, short bow (full draw in 14 ticks, softer arrows), goblin crown, goblin head, the four armor pieces and the bounty board. They have no recipes (except cooking the meat): get them from trades, bounties and loot.

  • Patrols: Goblin Spearmen and Archers patrol their village and watch the gates, and the Goblin Chief (40 health, drops the crown) sits in the den. A guard gives up a chase that leads too far from its village.
  • Hostility: Hurt a goblin, or open one of their chests, and the whole village turns hostile: the guards attack, trading and the board are refused. After 3 game days of peace they calm down (killing a goblin is remembered for 15 days).
  • The Crown: While you wear the goblin crown, every gift, trade or bounty gives 5 extra trust.

Supported mods: none yet—villages, goblins, trades and bounties use only vanilla blocks and job sites. Guards attack vanilla villagers and iron golems by default (switch it off in the config). Goblin Villages is the first mod of the Folkforge series; the library carries the village, reputation and trade systems that later themed mods share.

config/goblin_villages-startup.toml (read at startup; restart after changes):

  • guards.attackVanillaVillagers (true): guards attack vanilla villagers and iron golems.
  • guards.chaseBlocks (16): how far beyond the village a guard chases.
  • reputation.hostilityEnabled (true), reputation.hostilityDecayDays (3), reputation.killGuiltDecayDays (15).
  • crown.trustBonusEnabled (true) and armor.setTrustBonusEnabled (true): the extra trust of the crown and of a full armor set.
  • Worldgen: how often villages and camps generate is world data. Override data/goblin_villages/worldgen/structure_set/goblin_villages.json or hunters_camps.json with a datapack (default: villages like vanilla ones, camps rarer, never within 6 chunks of a goblin village).
  • Commands & eggs: Spawn eggs for every goblin are in the Spawn Eggs tab and in the Goblin Villages tab. Check the settlement with /folkforge themes and /folkforge doctor.

  • Known limits: Goblins say the villager "yes" and "no" when they accept a gift or refuse an item (Folkforge cannot change those yet). A goblin chest counts as opened by you even if no goblin saw it. Vanilla villagers may rarely take a goblin job site block. Goblins only go in out of the rain when they are idle. Villages made by an older version have no bounty board; new villages do.
