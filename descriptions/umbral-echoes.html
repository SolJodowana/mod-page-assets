An addon for Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks introducing the Mirror school of magic—built for the mage who commands no innate spells of their own, only everyone else's, alongside shadow tricks that need no enemy at all.

A Mirror mage doesn't throw ordinary fireballs—they watch what enemies cast, catch it as a shadow, hurl it back, and with enough practice bind it permanently into their own grimoire. When no enemy caster is around, the school steps through shadows, leaves decoys, stores incoming blows, and opens portals through mirrors.

Spell copying works seamlessly across both mobs and players for any spell built on Iron's Spells, including spells from third-party addons. Its six solo spells require no enemy caster, keeping the school fully viable in singleplayer or non-PvP worlds.

Supports Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge) (NeoForge 1.21.1 planned).

  1. Catch: Steal or intercept an enemy's spell using one of three distinct copying spells.
  2. Store: The stolen spell lands on an Echo Card inside your Mimic Grimoire with limited charges.
  3. Cast or Burn: Cast the copy to gain mastery points and tame the spell permanently—or burn every active card at once for raw elemental burst damage.

🪞 Four Spell-Stealing & Echo Spells

  • Echo (Uncommon): Point at a target to immediately replay the last spell it cast and save an Echo Card. A subtle crosshair indicator alerts you whenever a target has a catchable spell.
  • Mirror Ward (Uncommon): Conjures a black pane in front of you for a few seconds that reflects incoming spell projectiles back at their caster, saving the first reflected spell as a card.
  • Adaptation (Common): Brace for impact—the first spell that hits you is weakened, grants 30 seconds of resistance to its entire school, awards 3x learning progress, and saves as a card.
  • Unravel (Rare): Consumes all held Echo Cards in a shockwave burst matching each card's magic school. Burned cards grant zero learning progress—forcing a choice between casting to learn or burning to kill.

🌑 Six Solo Shadow Spells

  • Umbral Step (Common): Blink forward through shadow along your gaze—passing through thin walls—while leaving a dark decoy behind for 3 seconds.
  • Mirror Image (Uncommon): Spawns 2–4 dark decoys around you that draw hostile mob aggro and shatter upon taking a hit.
  • Reprise (Rare): Re-casts your own most recent instant spell at reduced power for half its normal mana cost (plus Reprise's own cost; generates no Echo Card).
  • Backlash (Epic): Absorbs and stores all incoming damage taken over 5 seconds, unleashing it in an area burst when the timer expires or when recast early.
  • Gloaming Veil (Uncommon): Grants true invisibility and silent footsteps until you attack or cast another spell.
  • Looking-Glass Door (Rare): Cast on two separate walls to link two misty black mirrors—walk into one and step directly out of the other (portals do not persist across server restarts).

Every spell you witness, endure, catch, or cast as a copy earns taming points toward that spell. Passive observation and taking hits only progress a spell up to Studied; reaching higher tiers requires actively catching and casting copies.

  • Permanent Binding: Once a spell reaches Attuned, you can spend experience levels to bind it permanently into your Mimic Grimoire. Bound copies automatically scale up when you reach Mastered, and can be cleared anytime via the Unbind button (bound copies cannot be converted into scrolls).

  • Mimic Grimoire: A specialized spellbook featuring 6 standard spell slots and 3 Echo Card slots. Sneak + right-click (or press your bound key) to inspect active cards, taming progress across all encountered spells, and the Bind menu.
    • Recipe: Book + 2 Crying Obsidian + Tinted Glass + Magic Cloth.
  • Looking Glass (Necklace): Look at any creature to display a floating status panel above its head showing spells it has recently cast, your taming progress on each, and what it is currently casting (can be moved near your hotbar via a client config toggle).
    • Recipe: Spyglass + Crying Obsidian + Amethyst Shard + Arcane Essence.

  • Shroud Armor (work in progress): Two hooded sets in the Umbral Echoes creative tab. Umbral adapts: each school of spell that hits you is learned, four hits fill the resistance (up to 40% with a full set), and it fades after 20 seconds. Mirror adapts too (up to 50%) and throws part of a hit back at the caster as Mirror damage, once every 5 seconds. Each piece also adds Mirror spell power and max mana. Recipe: Umbral uses the vanilla armor shapes with Arcane Cloth; a Mirror piece is the Umbral piece + 2 Crying Obsidian + Arcane Essence. The textures and models are placeholders and will change.
  • Obtaining Scrolls: All ten Mirror scrolls appear in standard Iron's Spells loot chests and can be crafted at the Scroll Forge using Crying Obsidian as the focus.
