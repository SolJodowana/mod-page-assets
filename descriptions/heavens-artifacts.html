Seven relics left behind by gods, angels, and demons. Equip one at a time in its dedicated Curios slot to reshape how you play—granting passive traits alongside an active power triggered by R (rebindable). All seven artifacts currently rest in an Unawakened state: potent already, yet only a glimpse of what they once were.

Supports Minecraft 26.1.2 (NeoForge) (Java 25 required).

⚠️ Work in Progress (v0.1.2): Balance values are initial estimates and playtesting is still ongoing. Please back up your worlds before updating and share feedback on anything that feels off!

Artifact Origin / Source Passive Effects Active Power & Cooldown

Crown of Withering Demonic (Wither) Immune to Wither; melee hits apply Wither II for 5s Wither Skull: Launches a skull toward your aim without breaking blocks (10s cooldown)

Seraph's Feather Angelic (End City chests) No fall damage Flight: Grants creative-style flight for 30s (120s cooldown)
Martyr's Halo Angelic (Ancient City chests) Regenerates 1 HP every 5s Last Grace: Automatically prevents a lethal blow once every 10 minutes, leaving you at 8 HP
Horn of the Fallen Demonic (Bastion Remnant treasure chests) Immune to fire and lava; +25% attack damage at 30% HP or lower Infernal Charge: Dashes forward and scorches enemies passed (15s cooldown)

  • Boss Drops: The Ender Dragon, Wither, Warden, and Elder Guardian always drop 1 matching shard and have a 10% chance to drop the complete artifact.
  • Shard Crafting: Combine 4 shards of the same kind in a 2x2 grid to craft the artifact—guaranteeing progression within four kills.
  • Rare Structure Chests: Seraph's Feather, Martyr's Halo, and Horn of the Fallen have no boss source; each has a 3% chance per chest inside its respective structure (End Cities, Ancient Cities, and Bastion Remnant treasure chests).

  • On Your Character:
    • Martyr's Halo hovers above your head and glows in the dark.
    • Crown of Withering and Horn of the Fallen sit on your head.
    • Dragon's Heart, Echo of the Abyss, and Eye of the Deep hang on your chest.
    • Seraph's Feather manifests as a pair of wings.
  • In Hand & Inventory: Artifacts appear as 3D models in hand, on the ground, and in item frames, while using clean 2D icons in inventories.
  • HUD & Keybinds: Your equipped artifact and active cooldown display in the bottom-right corner. Bind Use Artifact Power (R by default) in Controls—tooltips and HUD prompts update automatically.

🔧 Configuration & Known Limits

  • Server Config: Tune cooldowns, Flight duration, boss drop odds, and structure chest chances in the server config.
  • Known Limits:
    • Crafting recipes are hidden from the vanilla recipe book.
    • Using /kill on the Ender Dragon does not trigger boss drops.
    • The glowing outline from Echo of the Abyss is currently visible to all players.
