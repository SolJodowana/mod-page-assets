Every spell you cast goes into your atlas! A complete progression companion and living encyclopedia for Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks.

Cast a spell once to record it in your Atlas—accessible anytime via the Arcane Framework Codex Hub (H), its own dedicated hotkey (G), or a draggable shortcut icon pinned right onto your inventory screen. Designed both as an approachable guide for new mages and a deep progression system for veterans, it automatically detects every installed spell addon out of the box—and every individual mechanic can be toggled off in the configs.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge) and Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge).

  • Automatic Spell Discovery: Dynamically detects every school and spell from Iron's Spells and all installed addons at runtime. Cast a spell from a book, scroll, or weapon to unlock its entry with a toast notification (search and filters hide undiscovered spells to prevent spoilers).
  • Detailed Spell Cards: View descriptions, level scaling, mana costs, cooldowns, your personal mastery rank, acquisition sources (Scroll Forge recipes, loot tables, and items carrying the spell), and combat stats—complete with one-click JEI/EMI recipes, favorites, personal notes, and side-by-side spell comparisons.
  • My Spellbook & Live Statistics: Inspect equipped spells with real post-bonus values, and check total mana, spell power, cooldown/cast-time reduction, and per-school power/resistance (hover any stat to see the exact item or affix granting it).
  • Beginner's Mage Guide: Covers mana, scrolls, spellbooks, the Scroll Forge, runes, the Arcane Anvil, custom keybinds, per-school breakdowns, 30+ ready-made builds, "Next Steps" prompts, and subtle in-chat tips.
  • Rumors & Visual Themes: Track hints pointing toward undiscovered spells without spoiling their names, and switch between the Night Grimoire theme (with particle effects) and the Classic book style (Note: the Bestiary has moved to its own standalone mod while preserving your previous kill counts).
  • All-in-One Settings Tab: Configure every client option for Spell's Atlas—plus Artificer's Almanac and Tempest Almanac when installed—including spellbook/back slots, HUD elements, and keybindings in one unified menu.

  • Spell Mastery (Ranks I–III): Repeatedly casting a spell raises its mastery rank, boosting its effective level and unlocking a selectable perk: Power, Thrift, or Haste.
  • 32 Elemental School Reactions: Strike enemies with two magic schools in quick succession to trigger combo effects—such as Thermal Shock (Fire + Ice) or Overload (Fire + Lightning), including full support for addon schools and cooperative multiplayer combos.
  • School Affinity & Familiars: Choose a primary school and two secondary schools to earn passive power, mana efficiency, and cooldown bonuses. At 25% affinity, a School Familiar joins you—an evolving 4-tier spirit that assists in combat with school-themed magic (Fire burns, Ice slows, Holy heals).
  • Knowledge & Scroll Study: Earn permanent passive bonuses by completing school entries, hunting specific magic mobs, or studying spare spell scrolls.
  • Earnable Mage Titles: Unlock prefixes ranging from Apprentice and Archmage to School Master, Grandmaster, Alchemist, and Monster Hunter—each granting a small active bonus while equipped.

  • Arcane Points: Earned naturally through spell discoveries, mastery milestones, elemental reactions, challenges, elites, and boss kills.
  • The Perk Shop: Spend points across 36 perks spanning five categories (Attack, Defense, Summons, Mana, Utility) and five rarities, equipping them into unlockable perk slots alongside perk cards and slot sigils.
  • Purely Cosmetic Unlocks: Customize your title colors, spellcasting particle trails, and Atlas cover tints—unlocked permanently with zero impact on combat balance.

  • Daily & Weekly Server Challenges: Shared server-wide objectives rewarding loot, experience, and Arcane Points.
  • Scaling Difficulty & Elites: Hostile mobs scale dynamically alongside your magical progression, spawning dangerous Elite variants.
  • Magical Anomalies: School-aligned rifts that tear open in the world, spawning waves of enemies and a guardian across four difficulty tiers.
  • The Nemesis System: Any monster that defeats you claims a unique name, rank, and epithet, retreating to a lair where it grows stronger, ambushes you, and yields hefty rewards once defeated—immortalized in the Hall of the Fallen.
  • Safe Mage Duels & Gear Sets: Challenge other players directly from your inventory for non-lethal duels (ending at 1 HP with zero item loss) tracked via an Elo rating system, and equip matching armor sets for school-wide bonuses.
