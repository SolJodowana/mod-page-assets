Create custom, fully modular spellbooks for Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks! Hunt mobs, tan their hides, and craft unique covers, pages, and bindings tailored to your exact playstyle. A built-in guide book, the Almanac, explains every part.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge) and 1.20.1 (Forge).

  • Hunt & Tan: Slay mobs (wolves, spiders, endermen, villagers, Warden) to collect hides and rare trophies, then process them on the Tanning Rack.
  • Binding Table Crafting: Combine tanned materials with Ink and Arcane Essence to forge modular book components:
    • 📔 Cover: Sets magic school focus and Spell Power.
    • 📄 Pages: Determines total Spell Slots.
    • 🧵 Binding: Boosts Max Mana, Cooldown Reduction, or Cast Speed.
    • 🔒 Clasp (Optional): Adds passive bonuses, gem sockets, or double-edged curses.
  • Workshop Look: The binding table is a small workshop with a book press, a spool of thread and an ink bottle. Tanning racks hold a stretched hide, and every skin is drawn as a pelt with its own pattern.
  • Dynamic 3D Visuals: Crafted books display layered 3D models with distinct covers, bindings, clasps, and emblems whether held, equipped on hips, or placed on lecterns.
  • The Almanac Guide Book: Craft a book and a piece of leather to get the Almanac, or open it from the Codex Hub of the Arcane Framework next to the other Almanac books. It lists parts, rarity, sets, skins, tanning and unbinding in one leather-bound window.
  • Unbind & Recycle: Disassemble any custom book at the Binding Table to recover exact parts, or break down standard Iron's Spells books into components and spell scrolls.
  • Dual-School & Holy Covers: Strider skin makes a Fire + Ice cover, camel skin a Holy cover. Unique Iron's books give relic clasps that keep their spell, and upgrade orbs come back when you unbind.
  • 60+ Materials & Set Bonuses: Craft components across 5 rarities and unlock matching school or mob family set bonuses. Shift-hover to compare stats with equipped books.

📌 Requirements

  • Arcane Framework (0.0.1+ | Client & Server)
  • Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks (3.16+ | Client & Server)
  • Curios API (Client & Server)

📚 Same Author Mods

  • Spell's Atlas — Grants +1 Arcane Point for the first crafted component of each kind, adds binding challenges and four titles.
  • Artificer's Almanac — Adds extra bindings, unique clasps, and Arcane Dust when unbinding external books.
  • Tempest Almanac — Integrates elemental leather and weather-based crafting components.

🧩 Optional Mod Integrations

  • Recipes & Guides: JEI / EMI (recipe pages for binding, unbinding & tanning rates; EMI on NeoForge only for now), Patchouli (in-game guide book)
  • World Info & HUD: Jade / WTHIT / The One Probe (real-time tanning progress and lectern book schools; NeoForge only for now)
  • Sockets & Customization: Apotheosis (epic clasps provide gem sockets), KubeJS (custom materials & drop logic)

  • Datapack Driven: Easily adjust material values, drop rates, component balance, and caps using server configs or datapacks.
  • Multi-Language: Includes full built-in translations for English and Polish.
