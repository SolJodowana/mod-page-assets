Every creature in your world, vanilla or modded, can become your spirit companion! Win the trust of a cow, subdue the fury of a zombie, or gather the essence of a squid, and a translucent spirit of that species will float beside you, granting subtle stat bonuses and asking only for a treat now and then.

Familiar's Almanac is a standalone companion mod with its own book in the Arcane Framework Codex Hub (H), right next to the rest of the Almanac series.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge) and Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge).

🎨 New in 0.0.3: the Roster screen follows the GUI theme you pick in the Arcane Framework settings: Night, Ivory Ink, Graphite Copper or Amethyst Dusk.

  • The Pact:
    • Passive & Neutral Mobs: Sneak + right-click a creature with its favorite food to build trust. Once trusted, use a Pact Sigil to form a bond.
    • Hostile Mobs: Monsters do not trust easily. Weaken them below 25% health first, then use a Pact Sigil. The creature itself is never harmed or altered by the ritual.
  • Soul Essences: Defeated creatures occasionally drop species-specific Essences. Gather eight matching essences and bind them at a Familiar's Hearth (essences of different species never mix).

  • Universal 3D Rendering: Your familiar renders using the creature's actual 3D model, scaled down with a soft translucent tint, and works automatically for any vanilla or modded living entity. If a custom mod model cannot be rendered safely, a rotating amethyst shard appears in its place so the game never crashes.
  • Intangible & Safe: Spirits hover peacefully at your side; they cannot be hurt, blocked by arrows, or touched by other players.

  • 5 Bond Levels: Spending time together, defeating foes and offering treats levels your bond from Level 1 to Level 5.
  • Flavor Over Meta: Every species grants the exact same total stat budget, spread according to what the creature naturally excels at: swift mobs grant Movement Speed, sturdy ones Max Health, lucky ones Luck. Pick your favorite companion without worrying about min-maxing!
  • Level 3 Perks & Wardsight: Fire-immune creatures grant Fire Resistance, flyers Slow Falling and aquatic mobs Water Breathing. Every Level 3+ familiar also highlights hostile mobs within 12 blocks with a subtle glow (Wardsight).
  • Dual Companions (Level 5): Reaching Level 5 unlocks a second active familiar slot, so two spirits can follow you at once.
  • Transparent Attributes: All bonuses use standard attribute modifiers visible on every stats screen, with a configurable total cap.

  • Simple Upkeep: A familiar's mood slowly decreases while summoned. Restore it by right-clicking your spirit with its favorite food or by resting near a Familiar's Hearth.
  • Forgiving Mechanics: A worn-out familiar stays by your side and simply pauses its stat bonuses until rested. Bonds are permanent unless you enable Hard Mode in the server config.

  • Multiple Ways to Open: Open your book from the Arcane Framework Codex Hub (H), a dedicated hotkey, the physical book item, or a pinned icon beside your inventory.
  • Interactive Collector Cards: Browse discovered species cards with live 3D models, bond levels and mood. Click any card to inspect its stat distribution, Level 3 perk, favorite foods and the built-in guide, and summon, dismiss or swap active familiars right from the page.

All bonds, levels, mood values and attribute modifiers are managed and validated on the server. Clients only render synced state, and only a familiar's owner can feed, summon or dismiss it.

📌 Required Mods (Install on both Client & Server)

  • NeoForge: Minecraft 1.21.1 + NeoForge 21.1.250+ (Java 21 required)
  • Forge: Minecraft 1.20.1 + Forge 47.4.10+ (Java 17 required)
  • Arcane Framework 0.0.4+ (Required library providing the Codex Hub and book reader)

  • Zero-Setup Modded Support: Any modded mob without a custom file derives its favorite foods, stat weights and perks from its public entity attributes.
  • Datapack Customization: Override or define species behavior via data/<namespace>/familiars_almanac/species/<entity>.json (favorite_foods, weights, perks), and control exclusions with the entity type tags familiars_almanac:blacklist and familiars_almanac:no_essence.
