Your chests stay chests. The network just learns where they are.

Place a Core Pylon, and every chest, barrel, and modded container in range joins a wireless network. Open one Arcane Interface to search, sort, craft, and brew straight from your storages. Nothing is copied into a hidden database—your items stay safely inside the real blocks you built.

Supports Minecraft 26.1.2 on NeoForge and Fabric (two files on the Files tab).

💬 Author's Note & Work in Progress:
This mod is actively developed and designed primarily with casual players in mind—meaning it is intentionally accessible and straightforward to set up in the early game, while scaling cleanly to stay genuinely useful through the mid-game and endgame.
Because the project is still evolving, block models, textures, and visual themes may change in future updates (always back up your worlds before updating!). I am completely open to suggestions, constructive criticism, and new feature ideas—leave a comment below and let me know what you think!

  • Built for Casual & Relaxed Play: No complex channels, voltage math, or controller mazes. Drop a pylon, link an interface, and enjoy organized storage right from the start of your playthrough.
  • Real Physical Storage: Vanilla chests, barrels, and modded containers keep their items. Unloaded chunks simply appear offline without force-loading chunks.
  • All-in-One Window: View all items, individual storage tabs, crafting, brewing, and gear loadouts inside a single unified screen.
  • Smart Storage Rules: Assign custom names, priorities, item filters, or overflow flags per container so items sort themselves automatically.
  • Three Network Themes: Owners can switch the visual style of all blocks and windows for every member between Study (brass & green leather), Scriptorium (marble & silver), and Workshop (brick & copper).
  • Safe & Illustrated: Server-validated transfers prevent item loss, while the built-in Arcane Guide illustrates every block recipe.

  1. Craft a Resonance Crystal and click a Core Pylon to attune your network.
  2. Place chests within range—they connect automatically.
  3. Attune an Arcane Interface with your crystal and right-click to open.
  4. Expand with Relay Pylons and upgrades as you progress from early-game iron and amethyst to endgame Nether Stars.

🧱 Core Blocks

  • Core Pylon: Heart of the network; right-click to install upgrades. Breaking it puts the network to sleep without losing data.
  • Relay Pylon: Two-block pylon that extends network coverage to distant rooms.
  • Arcane Interface: Standing or wall-mounted terminal featuring full search, filtering, and network settings.
  • Arcane Screen: Wall display showing live item icons and counts. Adjacent screens merge up to 8x8 and auto-turn pages.
  • Arcane Chest & Arcane Vault: Dedicated storage blocks featuring automatic detection and a live fill-level gauge on the Vault door.

💎 Items & Upgrades

  • Tools: Resonance Crystal (device binding), Arcane Storage Label (rename storage tabs via Anvil), Arcane Focus (remote access wand), and Arcane Guide (given on first join).
  • Modules: Resonance Amplifier (range), Storage Matrix (capacity), Crafting & Alchemical Matrices (network crafting/brewing tabs), Equipment Organizer (1-click gear loadouts), Remote Attunement, and Dimensional Anchor (cross-dimension access).

  • Range-Extending Blocks: New dedicated placeable blocks to boost network coverage across larger bases.
  • Visual & Texture Polish: Refined block textures, smoother animations, and brand-new magical particle/visual effects.
  • New Block Variants: Additional aesthetic and functional variants for network blocks to fit more building styles.
  • Alternative Crafting Recipes: New craftable variants using alternative ingredients so you can get started easily even when amethyst is hard to find in vanilla worldgen.

  • Access Control: Networks can be set to Private, Shared with specific players, or Public (guests use the network with settings locked).
  • Modded Containers: Reads standard inventories automatically; custom adapters can be registered via the public API.
  • Chunk Loading: Does not force-load chunks. Unloaded storages reconnect automatically when approached.
