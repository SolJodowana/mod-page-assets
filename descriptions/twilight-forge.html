An addon for The Twilight Forest! Combine realm materials in a heat-powered forge block to smelt new metal alloys, craft custom armor and tools with set bonuses, and upgrade your existing Twilight Forest gear at a smithing table.

Supports Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge) and Minecraft 26.1.2 (NeoForge).

  • Heat-Tiered Twilight Forge Block: Combine up to six ingredients into alloy ingots. Hotter fuels unlock higher-tier recipes (Heat I: any fuel, Heat II: coal or charcoal, Heat III: Fiery Blood or Fiery Tears — hover the heat pips to inspect). Fully compatible with hoppers and pipes for automation.
  • Custom Alloys, Armor & Tools: Smelt and forge full gear sets across six alloys: Bronzewood, Leafsteel, Emberknight, Frostsilver, Twilight Alloy, and the endgame Zenithium (a near-black teal metal with a cold green glow, forged from all five other alloys plus a Netherite Ingot).
  • Toggleable Set Bonuses: Equip full armor sets to gain unique passives: Speed, Regeneration, Fire Immunity, Frost Armor, or Extra Hearts. A full Zenithium set grants all five bonuses simultaneously!
  • Seamless Smithing Upgrades: Upgrade existing Twilight Forest gear using alloy ingots and Upgrade Templates at the Smithing Table while preserving all enchantments, custom names, and durability states.
  • Fully Configurable: Fine-tune every armor and tool stat to match your modpack's balance.
