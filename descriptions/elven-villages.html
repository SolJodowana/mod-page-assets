Treetop elven settlements nestled high in the crowns of giant trees! Climb ropes and vines into canopy villages connected by bridges, trade for silver leaves, earn local reputation, or face elven archers and blade guards.

Supports Minecraft 26.1.2 (NeoForge).

⚠️ Work in Progress (v0.0.2): This mod is under active development—voice lines are currently synthesized drafts, and treetop buildings/interiors will receive visual upgrades in future updates. Always back up your worlds before updating!

  • Biome-Adapted Canopy Villages: Great trees joined by bridges featuring a Heart Tree (Elder's hall & lantern square), home trees, workshops, and outer archer nests. Wood types adapt to biomes (Oak, Birch, Jungle, and Spruce), with dynamic roots reaching the soil.
  • Custom Models & Daily Routines: Slim elven models with four hair colors and biome-tinted skin tones. Elves work at job sites by day, gather around lanterns at dusk, sleep in beds, and raise children who inherit their parents' trades.
  • Climbable Rope Block: Craft from string or buy from Weavers. Hangs from blocks, extends downward with a right-click, and works underwater.
  • Dedicated Audio & Tab: Custom elven voice effects and a dedicated creative tab with spawn eggs.

Elves trade exclusively in Silver Leaves. All 5 professions utilize vanilla job site blocks:

Profession Job Site Primary Trade Goods
Herbalist Brewing Stand Flowers, seeds, nether wart, and healing, swiftness & regeneration potions.
Bowyer Fletching Table Arrows, bows, crossbows, enchanted bows, and the Elven Longbow.
Weaver Loom Rope, wool, carpets, banners, paintings, and item frames.
Beekeeper Barrel Honey, honeycombs, candles, Waybread, beehives, and bee nests.
Scribe Lectern Paper, books, enchanted books, maps, explorer maps, and compasses.

🛠️ Exclusive Loot, Gear & Food

  • Silver Leaf: Primary elven currency; crouch + right-click any elf to gift wanted items and boost trust.
  • Elven Longbow: Longer draw time (30 ticks), firing arrows 25% faster, farther, and harder.
  • Elven Glaive: Melee polearm with +1 block extended attack reach.
  • Waybread: Crafted from 3 wheat and 1 honey bottle (yields 2 high-saturation loaves).
  • Elder's Diadem: Helmet dropped by the Village Elder; grants bonus trust on every gift and trade (toggleable in config).

  • Canopy Defense: Archers snipe threats from outer nests, Blade Guards patrol bridges, and the Elder guards the Heart Tree. Guards stay up in the canopy and do not descend to the ground.
  • Reputation System: Harming an elf or opening village chests turns the entire settlement hostile. Hostility fades after 3 in-game days of peace. By default, archers and the Elder also shoot nearby monsters.
